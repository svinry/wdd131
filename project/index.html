A place shaped by water

One river. Two countries. A wonder worth slowing down for.

Known locally as Mosi-oa-Tunya, or “The Smoke That Thunders,” Victoria Falls is a vast curtain of water on the Zambezi River, shared by Zimbabwe and Zambia. Its spray, rainforest, basalt gorges, and rainbows make the landscape extraordinary. Read the UNESCO description.

Use this guide to pair a visit to the viewpoints with an activity that suits your pace—from a gentle river cruise to a full day outdoors. Conditions, access, and availability change by season; confirm details with official park staff and licensed operators before you go.

Settle in at your own pace

Where to stay

Victoria Falls town and nearby areas offer different styles of stays. These are starting points for your own search, not endorsements or bookings.

Lodges & guesthouses

Look for smaller stays if you value a personal welcome and local character. Check the exact location, meal options, and transfer arrangements.

Hotels & resorts

Compare larger properties for on-site services and facilities. Review cancellation terms and whether park transport is included.

Camping & self-catering

For more independent travel, verify current site facilities, safety arrangements, access, and any seasonal restrictions before booking.

Compare current guest reviews, location, accessibility, and transport needs; confirm details directly with your accommodation.

Make room for the moment

A little planning goes a long way.

Pack water and sun protection, wear comfortable walking shoes, and check current park guidance and activity conditions before setting out.
